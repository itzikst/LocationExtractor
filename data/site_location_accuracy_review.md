# Archaeological Water Installations Dataset: Location Accuracy Review

This document provides a complete audit of all **166 sites** in the dataset derived from Dr. Zvika Tzuk's PhD dissertation.
Sites are classified into 4 precision tiers for academic verification.

---

## 1. Summary of Location Precision Categories
- **Exact Archaeological Site / IAA Grid (118 sites)**: Pinpoint coordinates of excavated tells, khirbets, fortress cisterns, or springs in Israel.
- **International Comparative Sites (39 sites)**: Well-known ancient sites across the Levant and Mediterranean (Syria, Jordan, Greece, Egypt, Turkey, Mesopotamia, Italy).
- **Approximate Village / Spring Area (~8 sites)**: Smaller rural sites or valleys where the coordinates mark the ancient settlement area.
- **Regional Survey Area (9 sites)**: Centroids representing regional survey areas rather than single isolated installations.
